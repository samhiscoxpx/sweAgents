# Deferred features

Something missing goes here; something broken goes in `<defect-register>`. One line per entry,
`- [<where found>, <date>] <description>`: where found is the phase, such as `phase 3`, or the chore;
the date is read from the clock. Say what is missing and the failure its absence leaves possible.
When an entry lands, append a dated closing note and move the whole entry to the finished file; never
rewrite it.

## Open

Entries nobody has scheduled, newest last.

## In progress

Entries a named phase or chore is building; move an entry here when work on it starts.
