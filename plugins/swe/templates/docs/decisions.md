# Decisions waiting on <human-name>

The running batch of questions and decisions for the human principal, kept by the Co-CTO so nothing
waits on a session's memory. A number is given once and kept until it is answered or withdrawn; never
sort, merge or renumber entries, and keep each option's letter.

## Open

One entry per question: `- **<ID>** <the question, self-contained>. Raised by <session>, <date>.`
followed by its lettered options, each with its cost, and the recommended one marked `[REC]`.

## Overnight decisions

One entry per decision taken while the human principal was away: item ID, decision, reason, evidence,
affected work and reversal steps.

## Answered or withdrawn

Move an entry here with the answer, the date and where the decision is now recorded.
