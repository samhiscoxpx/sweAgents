# Defects

Something broken goes here; something missing goes in `<deferred-feature-register>`. One line per
entry, `- [<where found>, <date>] <description>`: where found is the phase, such as `phase 3`, or the
chore; the date is read from the clock. Say what was seen, where, and how it arises. When an entry
closes, append a dated closing note and move the whole entry to the finished file; never rewrite it.

## Open

Entries nobody is working on yet, newest last.

## In progress

Entries a named phase or chore is fixing; move an entry here when work on it starts.
